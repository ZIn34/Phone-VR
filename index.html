Phone VR

Hold your phone up in front of your eyes like a window, and look around.

How you play

Allow motion and camera when asked, then turn the phone sideways. You start in the home menu, in VR.

    Good to know: in Settings you can set how far the phone is from your face, so things look the right size. If you use a holder with lenses, turn on Lens holder there to get two pictures, one for each eye.

    Hide Safari's bars

    1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
    2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
    3. Open Phone VR from the new icon. It fills the whole screen.
