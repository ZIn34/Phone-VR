Phone VR

Hold your phone up to your eyes, or put it in a headset holder, and look around.

How you play

Open the menu in VR Start once, then choose everything from tiles floating in front of you.

Games

Follow the circle Keep your crosshair on a moving circle for one minute. It gets faster.

Testing

See your room Your back camera in both eyes, with head tracking markers. Pop the targets Try out your way of playing: side buttons, one hand or both hands.

Hide Safari's bars

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Phone VR from the new icon. It fills the whole screen.
