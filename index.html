See your room

Your phone's back camera, shown to both eyes.

  1. Tap Start.
  2. Allow motion and camera when asked.
  3. Turn the phone sideways, then hold it up to your eyes or put it in a holder.

    Good to know: a phone has one back camera, so both eyes get the same picture. You will see your room, but it will look flat, not truly 3D.
