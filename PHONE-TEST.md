# Phone test guide

Everything here needs a real iPhone, so it could not be tried on the computer
the page was built on. There, the layout, settings, problem messages, game
rules and maths were tested with a test picture, a pretend head and a pretend
hand. The real hand tracker was only run on a photo of two hands.

Link: https://zin34.github.io/Phone-VR/ (the capitals matter).

## 1. Start

1. Open the link in Safari. Pick **Level 1** under How you play.
2. Tap **Start**. Allow **motion**, then the **camera**.
3. Turn the phone sideways and hold it up in front of you at a normal
   distance. No lenses are needed. (Upright does not work: it asks you to
   turn the phone sideways.)

Works if: you see one big picture of your room from the back camera, with the
home menu's tiles floating in it.

To hide Safari's bars: Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open it from there.

## 2. The home menu, Level 1

1. Turn your head: the tiles should stay put in the room.
2. Look at a tile for a few seconds. **Nothing should be chosen.**
3. Put the cross on **Follow the circle** and tap **SELECT** at either side.

Works if: looking alone never chooses; SELECT chooses the tile under the cross.

## 3. Follow the circle

1. No buttons should be on the screen while you play.
2. Look at the orange circle until the ring fills, then follow it for a minute.
3. Tap the screen once: the buttons should appear for about four seconds.
   Tap **Exit** while they are showing to go back to the home menu.

Please tell me: too easy or too hard, too fast at the end, comfortable or not.

## 3b. Block world

1. From the home menu choose **Block world**. Your room should be gone: you
   are standing among blocks, with sky above.
2. Turn all the way round and look up and down.
3. Walk: take real steps (or step on the spot) and you move forward the way
   you are facing. At Level 1 you can also hold **WALK** (left thumb). At
   Levels 2 and 3 hold up **two fingers** in front of the camera.
4. The inventory bar along the bottom shows the blocks you can build with. At
   Level 1 tap one to choose it. At Levels 2 and 3 hold up **three fingers**
   to move to the next one.
5. Level 1: aim the cross at a block (white outline) and tap **BUILD** or
   **BREAK**. Levels 2 and 3: point at a block and **pinch** to build (keep
   your other fingers open); hold a **fist** over a block to break it.
6. Leave and come back: what you built should still be there.
7. Jump and fly. Level 1: tap **JUMP**; double tap it (or double tap the
   picture) to fly, then hold WALK and look where you want to go; double tap
   again to land. Levels 2 and 3: little finger up to jump; index and little
   finger up, held for a moment, to fly or land. Walking into a block one
   high hops you up it.
8. Health is top left. Animals (pigs, sheep) wander; green enemies come for
   you and take health when they reach you. Hit them with BREAK (Level 1) or
   a fist (Levels 2 and 3). Knocking out an animal gives health back.
9. Breaking a block gives you that block; the numbers in the bar are how
   many you have. Open the menu and choose **Craft** to turn them into
   planks, brick, glass or a lamp.
10. If moving the phone about makes you walk by mistake, turn off **Walk by
   stepping** in Settings.

Please tell me: is it smooth, do real steps move you (and does standing still
keep you still), do the two-finger and three-finger signs work, and is a stride
too long or too short.

## 3c. Blaster arena

1. From the home menu choose **Blaster arena**. You stand in an arena with
   walls. Robots hide behind them.
2. A robot steps out beside its wall, its eye turns red, and it fires one slow
   orange bolt at you. Walk aside (real steps, WALK, or two fingers) to dodge.
3. Level 1: put the sight on a robot and tap **FIRE**. Three hits knock it out.
   **NEXT** changes blaster once you have more than one.
4. Levels 2 and 3: hold your hand up as if holding a blaster. The blaster is
   drawn at your hand and the sight is where your hand is. **Curl your index
   finger** like pulling a trigger to fire. Three fingers up changes blaster.
5. Walk onto the coloured boxes lying in the arena to pick up the Rapid and
   Heavy blasters.
6. The health bar is top left. At zero it is game over: FIRE (or a trigger
   pull) plays again.

Please tell me: does the trigger pull fire when you mean it to and only then,
does the blaster follow your hand, are the bolts dodgeable, is it smooth.

## 4. Head tracking and eye fit

1. From the home menu choose **See your room**. The buttons stay on screen here.
2. Turn left: FRONT should slide right. Look up: the horizon should move down.
3. Line the white cross up with a door frame, look around for a minute, look
   back. The same green post should still be on it. If not, tap **Re-centre**.
4. Only if you ever get a holder with lenses: in the menu's Testing section
   choose **Headset view** for two pictures. Settings then also shows Eye
   distance, Picture size and Lens correction.

Please tell me: whether the green posts stay glued to real objects as you turn.

## 5. Levels 2 and 3 (hands)

1. Tap **Exit** at the home menu to get back to the start page. Pick
   **Level 2**, tap Start.
2. Hold the phone with one hand, keeping that hand out of the camera's view.
   Hold the other hand up in front, in good light.
3. Wait for "Getting hand tracking ready" to go. A white ring should sit on
   your index fingertip.
4. Point at a tile and **pinch** thumb and finger together. Or hold your
   fingertip on the tile for about a second.
5. In **Pop the targets**, touch a target with your fingertip.
6. To bring the menu back: hold your **open palm** up facing you, or hold a
   fingertip on the MENU button at the right of the view.
7. Level 3 is the same with the phone in a holder and both hands free.

Please tell me: did hand tracking start, how long it took, whether the view is
jerky, whether the ring sits on your fingertip, and whether pinch and palm work.
If hand tracking cannot start, SELECT buttons appear at the sides instead.

## Known limits

- Flat picture, not 3D: a phone has one back camera.
- Safari's bars only go away when opened from the Home Screen.
- The iPhone's motion reading has no compass, so FRONT can wander slowly.
- Hand tracking needs the hand in view of the camera and good light, and is
  heavy work for a phone. The first use downloads about 21 MB.
- Not tried on Android.
